# strategy-consulting-in-telecom-for-installment-financing
Some members of the BeyondTech executive team (particularly the Chief Financial Officer) believe that their pricing strategy and sales model is outdated and that they should implement installment financing, a pricing model that allows customers to pay for their purchase over time rather than all at once.

Question that need to be asked:

-Is installment financing a viable option for BeyondTech? 

-If yes, how has installment financing impacted the financial position of the client’s competitors?

-What are customers feeling about payment plans?


There is excitement from the leadership team, but they have also shared some concerns:

-Will a shift negatively impact brand perception? 

-Could this hurt profits if customers default on payments?

-Do they have the right technology and staff to support something like this?

S-1) Market research

S-2) Financial Modeling of competitor

S-3) Design a Survey

S-4) Data Analysis

S-5) Perspective of Stakeholder

S-6) Final Summary of insights
